# R-A1 frozen-candidate failure-factor decision

Date: 2026-07-29 CST

## Decision

The complete 24-frame diagnostic identifies confidence ranking and exact-10k
selection as a major R-A1/WCE bottleneck. It does not reopen R-A1 and does not
turn the validation-GT arm into a method result.

The diagnostic reconstructed the formal epoch-20 matched Q0+Q1 path with the
same checkpoint, 700,000 candidates, fixed `8,000/1,700/300` output quotas,
and 5 cm exclusion. Every current-confidence export and query tensor matched
the archived formal hashes exactly. Ground truth was introduced only after
candidate generation and only to define an unattainable nearest-distance
ranking over the frozen pool.

| Exact-10k arm | Mean Chamfer | Median completeness | Mean outlier | Far completeness |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Formal current confidence | `4.00895 m` | `1.50805 m` | `31.8129%` | `11.12111 m` |
| Validation-GT nearest score | `0.64102 m` | `0.14895 m` | `2.2804%` | `0.70414 m` |

The GT-nearest diagnostic passes all four frozen absolute geometry checks over
24 validation frames and all 23 far-target frames. The formal current arm
continues to fail Chamfer and outlier. The formal decision branch is therefore
`confidence_ranking_bottleneck_indicated`.

## Failure localization

- Frozen candidate-to-target mean distance: `13.3076 m`.
- Frozen target-to-candidate mean distance: `0.14858 m`.
- Current confidence versus negative candidate distance:
  Pearson `0.29389`, Spearman with average ties `0.54851`.
- Mean candidate distance in the top-confidence subsets:
  `1%: 1.2074 m`, `5%: 1.9778 m`, `10%: 2.6104 m`,
  `25%: 4.3554 m`.

The wide field covers the target well, but most candidates are poor. The
training objective and confidence head do not rank the small useful subset
strongly enough for the frozen quota-constrained export. Extending the same
training recipe is not authorized.

This result supports bounded R-A2 pilots that change supervision or sampling:

1. an eight-frame, 500-update memorization gate;
2. source-faithful RaLD classwise supervision as a control;
3. range-balanced positives with global and surface-shell negatives;
4. only after those gates, a confidence target aligned to geometric distance.

Failure to memorize closes the WCE field architecture. A pilot pass does not
promote a geometry parent; it must still improve the unchanged 24-frame
validation geometry and condition-use gates.
