# STDA-F0 pre-idea challenge draft

> Candidate: parity-packed sparse target-demand bipartite assignment  
> State: revised pre-freeze; no implementation or scientific result

## Bottleneck

Fresh-WCE contains strong aggregate geometric support, but the F0R pointwise
GT-nearest export retained all target-bearing range strata on only `2/12`
frames. VRH-F0 then showed that an active ordered renewal frontier was worse
than flat exposure and that both arms failed the complete first/later gate.
The next falsifiable question is set allocation under exact count and spacing,
not another pointwise score, fixed range quota, or renewal order.

## Hypothesis

A target-demand-coupled sparse assignment may spend the 10,000-point budget
across target support more effectively than either independent pointwise
ranking or a local greedy allocation. Each demand slot requires one point and
each packed candidate has unit capacity.

This is a graph-constrained rectangular assignment, not a new optimal-
transport algorithm. Euclidean XYZ KNN defines the graph; ray/range is used
only for physical evaluation. The optimization primitive is prior art and can
only serve as an internal capacity mechanism for a later Full-RAED generator.

## Capacity mechanism

1. In a target-free process, reconstruct all 700k Fresh-WCE candidates and
   commit one largest-cardinality 5 cm parity support before any target access.
2. In a separate process, canonicalize positive target mass independently of
   input row order and generate exactly 10,000 centered systematic slots.
3. Build a fixed `K=256` Euclidean KNN graph from slots to packed support with
   explicit boundary ties and positive int64 costs.
4. Certify full cardinality with SciPy and independent Hopcroft-Karp solvers.
5. Solve the graph-constrained sparse full assignment and compare it with a
   packed pointwise control and a target-atom round-robin greedy control.
6. Replay immutable bytes and evaluate strict count, complete exact float32
   5 cm enumeration, Chamfer, outlier, range strata, and all six
   representation-neutral `range x {first,later}` classes.

The target-conditioned graph is an oracle sidecar and is never deployable.

## Closest closed routes

- F0R selected independently by nearest-target distance on the full 700k
  candidate field; STDA imposes target demand and candidate capacity.
- VRH-F0 used one ordered renewal stream; STDA has no return frontier or STOP
  process.
- G1F used a failed 32k proposal pool and balanced-transport follow-up; STDA's
  capacity gate starts from one frozen largest-color parity subset derived from
  the independently supported Fresh-WCE field, not freely from all 700k points.

The parity support itself can discard useful candidates. A failure closes only
this frozen support/demand/graph/cost recipe.

## Controls and anti-win conditions

- decision and greedy share support, slots, graph, and integer costs;
- packed pointwise shares support and target, but not slots or graph;
- archived F0R full-700k pointwise export is descriptive history only;
- no best-of-color, adaptive K, radius expansion, range quota, dropped frame,
  padding, duplicate, jitter, validation, test, Doppler, or temporal access;
- all 76 training frames run once after freeze and must pass independently;
- assignment utility requires both matched controls to fail a corresponding
  gate passed by the decision arm.

## Strongest rejection case

The GT-fitted demand can expose only oracle capacity. Even if STDA passes, Cube
evidence may not predict the target allocation. Conversely, a graph Hall
deficit or decision geometry failure may be caused by parity packing, target-
confidence discretization, fixed `K=256`, or the composite integer cost rather
than by an impossibility of structured allocation.

The sparse graph is not equivalent to dense assignment, the solver is not
claimed mathematically unique, and no F0 outcome supports an algorithmic
novelty claim.

## Promotion and abandonment

- full-cardinality decision passes all absolute gates on `76/76`, while packed
  pointwise and greedy each fail a corresponding gate: authorize a separately
  frozen target-free Cube-conditioned learnability gate;
- packed pointwise also passes: retain packed support only;
- greedy also passes while pointwise fails: retain simple demand allocation;
- parity support has fewer than 10,000 rows: close the frozen parity-support
  recipe;
- graph cardinality fails: close the frozen sparse graph recipe;
- full-cardinality decision fails geometry: close the frozen assignment recipe;
- implementation/resource failure: repair and rerun the identical protocol.

No outcome directly unlocks Doppler, cycle, temporal, validation, or test work.
